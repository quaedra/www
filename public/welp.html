Welp

Welp-35B-A3B is a mixture-of-experts model with its experts quantized to ternary weights, so it runs entirely on a 16 GB GPU with a 262k context.

How it works

Welp is post-training quantization only: no fine-tuning and no new capabilities. The routed experts, most of the model's weights, are stored as ternary weights (PTQ1_0, 1.75 bits per weight). Each expert is fitted to minimize its output error, then refined layer by layer with GPTQ and block-wise scale tuning. The expert down-projections are kept at Q2_K and everything outside the experts at Q4_K.

At the same size, Welp beats 2-bit quantization on every quality measure here, at slightly lower speed. It is about level with Bonsai 2 27B on quality at more than twice the speed, and the larger UD-IQ3_XXS still scores higher on a 16 GB card.

Run it

Welp is a research build and hasn't been released. It needs PrismML's llama.cpp fork, which adds the ternary format, plus a local patch.

llama-server -m welp-9.4g.gguf -ngl 99 -fa on -c 262144 \
  -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0 --jinja
